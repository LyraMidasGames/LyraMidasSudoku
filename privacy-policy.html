Privacy Policy

Lyra Midas Sudoku · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Lyra Midas Sudoku ("the game") is published by LyraMidas Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

The game has no account system. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The player name and avatar you choose in the game are stored only on your device.

2. Game data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, coins, crystals and purchases are saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data. If you delete the game, this data is deleted with it.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners. To show and measure ads, these partners may collect device information such as:

  • the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android),
  • IP address, device model, operating system version and language,
  • approximate (country-level) location,
  • how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK, and under Apple's App Tracking Transparency on iOS), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in your device settings: on iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, on Android under Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Buying "No Ads" removes interrupting ads. Ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a reward remain available.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the game sends local reminders (for example, that the Daily Sudoku is ready). They are scheduled on your device, no data is sent to us, and they can be switched off in the game settings or in your device settings.

6. Diagnostics

The app stores, Apple and Google may collect crash reports and usage statistics about the app according to your device settings and their own privacy policies. We may see these reports in anonymous, aggregated form to fix problems.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, Turkey or California), you may have the right to access, delete or restrict the use of your data, and to withdraw consent to personalised advertising. Because the game keeps your data only on your device, deleting the game removes it completely. For data held by advertising partners, reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings, or contact us and we will help you.

9. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game. Advertising partners keep the data they collect according to their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address, with the date of the last change at the top.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please write to lyramidasgames@gmail.comL.
