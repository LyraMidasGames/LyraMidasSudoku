Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Fill the board so that every row, column and box contains each number exactly once. Tap a cell, then tap a number. Use the pencil to write notes. Each mistake costs a heart. When you run out of hearts you can continue with extra hearts or try the level again.

What do the boosters do?

Tap the "?" button on the game screen to see what each booster does. Quick Assist fills a random empty cell, Master Hint fills the cell you choose, Notes Master writes all correct notes, and Focus shows where a number can go.

I bought "No Ads" but I see ads again.

Open the Shop and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom, using the same Apple or Google account you bought it with. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available even with "No Ads".

My purchase did not arrive.

Close and reopen the game with a working internet connection. If the item still does not appear, write to us with the order number from your store receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the game or resetting the device removes it, and we cannot restore it because we do not store it on a server.

How do I turn off reminders, sounds or vibration?

Open Settings (the gear on the main screen). You can switch sounds, vibration and each reminder on or off there.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game from your device; all game data is stored there and is removed with it. To limit data used for ads, reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
